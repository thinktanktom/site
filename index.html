Thomas Cyriac — software engineer and independent researcher

I’m Thomas, a software engineer and independent researcher based in Coimbatore, India, with six years of experience across Web3, AI and networking. From 2021 to 2026 I was the lead smart contract engineer on BankX, a stablecoin protocol deployed across eight blockchains and audited by CoinFabrik (opens in new tab). I’ve also built self-hosted AI agent platforms for clients and SD-WAN tooling for MikroTik routers.

Most of my work happens directly with the people who need it and I deeply enjoy being close to where the problem gets defined. If you have something to build, or an idea to pressure-test, email me at thomas@thinktanktom.com or find me on Upwork (opens in new tab).

Outside work, I play amateur football, surf and teach yoga as a certified instructor. I read widely across fiction, history and research, and write essays that untangle technology and mathematics for people who find them overwhelming.
