Every developer eventually builds their own website. I've put mine off longer than I care to admit — not from lack of things to say, but from the uncomfortable truth that building something for yourself demands a different kind of honesty than building it for a client.

## Who I am

I'm Thomas Cyriac. I go by thinktanktom online. I've spent the last few years deep in DeFi protocol development: Solidity on Ethereum and its L2s, Rust for Soroban on Stellar, and generally thinking hard about how financial primitives can be rebuilt on open, permissionless infrastructure.

My flagship project has been [BankX](https://bankx.io) — a dual-token, silver-pegged algorithmic stablecoin that I've been lead developer on since late 2021. It's been audited by CoinFabrik and Immunefi, deployed across six chains, and taught me more about system design under adversarial conditions than anything else I've worked on.

## Open source

I contribute to open source when I find gaps worth filling. A few notable PRs over the past year: a utility library for OpenZeppelin's Soroban helpers, cross-chain tooling for Chainlink CCIP, CI action fixes for Ethereum Optimism, and some work on post-quantum consensus research at LambdaClass. Open source is where the real thinking happens — your code gets read by strangers who have no obligation to be kind about it, which is exactly the feedback loop you need.

## What I'll write about

This site is where I'll write about protocol design, smart contract architecture, and the specific class of problems that only appear when real money is on the line. Expect deep dives with code blocks for anything that needs them. No fluff, no thought leadership — just technical writing by someone who spends most of their time in a text editor and an EVM debugger.

If you're building something in DeFi and need a protocol engineer, you can [find me on Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/thinktanktom). Otherwise, I'm just here to write.

Welcome.
