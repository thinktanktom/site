Bubble Wrap

metawritingcultureindia

I built this website on a whim¹ (see note 1). Portfolio pieces are something I have always shied away from. It is not from a lack of trying, mind you. I used to occasionally pick up a project for the sole purpose of making myself look good, but I scarcely ever finished it. I've come to realise that I simply cannot tolerate performative engineering. Part of this was a choice, but much of it was friction: the hard work and introspection a project demands has always stood as a buffer between wishful thinking and execution, sparing me the hours I would otherwise squander on ideas that never deserved them.

With the advent of AI, that buffer has thinned considerably. The first few iterations of this website were built entirely with Claude² (see note 2), and they were mediocre at best. My original intention was to establish a foothold on the internet and vouch for my aptitude in system design and computer science with sloppy AI-generated content, open-source contributions and side projects.

But ever so slowly, like a grumpy parent who has had their infant son's yipping puppy thrust upon them, I found this website growing on me. In my pursuit of being performative, I had built something I loved, and I simply could not bring myself to feed it market-standard dog food any longer.

The life of an engineer is rather Harry Potter-esque. We surround ourselves with fellow technowizards, startup sorcerers and VC witches. Our YouTube and social media feeds overflow with the latest venture-funded techno babble and techbro rhetoric. Rarely do we come to notice the existence of people outside this bubble. Rarer still do we actually interact with them.

I find muggles³ (see note 3), as my colleagues would think of them, fascinating. They exist in their own bubbles and being that they are our original⁴ (see note 4) market, I think it is imperative that we bridge the gap between our cultures and better understand the impact of what we are building.

Fortunately, I have a great deal of experience with bubbles, most of it courtesy of being Indian.

Nothing you read on social media or glimpse in short-form content will ever convey the reality that is India. For all our stumbles over the years, we endure as an anthropological marvel. By sheer weight of population, religion and culture flourish in every nook and cranny. You will find entire swathes of people living by perspectives that would baffle the average observer, kind of like lifting your coffee mug to discover an entire ant colony on your kitchen table.

In fact, I often think of India as one enormous sheet of bubble wrap. Each perspective sealed in its own bubble, all completely different translations of the same primal themes and stories. Those with access to these perspectives and cultures rarely have the unbiased detachment needed to accurately report or translate these customs universally. They live in their own worlds, largely unaware of how they appear to those around them.

Sound familiar?

Translating one bubble to another, clearing up the bottleneck that complexity creates so that the average reader gains not just information, but understanding — this is my niche. This is what I intend to write about.

After all, we are each wizards in our own domain, and muggles in everyone else's.
